About Us

We are storytellers using scent as our medium. Since 2020, Nusantara Fragrance has been crafting premium artisan perfumery that reflects Indonesia's extraordinary natural richness.

Our Mission

To bring Indonesia's finest aromatic heritage to the world, supporting local growers and artisans while creating world-class fragrances that stand proudly alongside international houses.

Our Ingredients

We source oud from the deep forests of Kalimantan, sandalwood from Nusa Tenggara, jasmine from the highland gardens of East Java, and patchouli from the rolling hills of North Sumatra.

Our Team

Our master perfumers blend traditional Indonesian knowledge with modern European-trained technique, creating compositions that are both timeless and contemporary.

Our Values

Sustainability, authenticity, and craftsmanship are at the core of everything we do. We believe in ethical sourcing and giving back to the communities that cultivate our ingredients.
